## W Magazine — "Kali Uchis Makes Music Without Fear" (Sin Miedo era, 2020)
URL: https://www.wmagazine.com/story/kali-uchis-sin-miedo-interview

**Genre/Sound:**
- "I always want to be able to show a range of different emotions and the diversity of all things that inspire me."
- "I never really cared about what genre the music was or what language was being sung."

**Creative Philosophy:**
- "I am very much a creature of spontaneity and intuition. I don't like things that are overly calculated."
- "I look at life in general as like ripping off a Band-Aid, especially when I create music."
- "The point of art isn't about how people are going to receive it. The point is for it to be for me."

**Production Process:**
- "I traveled the world to make both [albums]...started production in Miami, then Los Angeles, and refined in London."
- "I'm just going to make what I want to make and I'm going to make something timeless."

**Influences:**
- "Jazz and soul music were my grounding for how I learned to produce and write."
- "I have to push myself to stay away from jazzy or soulful melodies because naturally I am drawn to do that."

**Bilingual:**
- "Mixing English and Spanish together in my songwriting can be very difficult...I really had to push myself to combine the languages."

**Career Vision:**
- "I never wanted a soaring rise to fame. I want a silent ascent to iconic."

## GRAMMY.com — "How A Bicultural Mindset, Working Alone & Embracing Her 'Bitchier Side' Resulted In Her Most Energetic Album" (Orquideas, 2024)
URL: https://www.grammy.com/news/kali-uchis-interview-orquideas-new-album-videos

**Genre/Sound:**
- "I feel like others see Latin music or Latin artists as just one thing. That's why I need to demonstrate all the different genres of Latin music."
- "[The whole album is] really a risk because it's not one genre; it's a mix of so many different things."
- "I have so much creativity that I need to express all the time. It's a little difficult for me to commit to just one sound, project, and language."

**Creative Process:**
- "[By working alone] I get a lot of my essence in music, which I feel is what people can really take away from it."
- "I don't do music [for the numbers]; I never feel pressure. I do this because it's my creative outlet."
- "I'd rather not go to radio than change my song."

**Bilingual Writing:**
- "I didn't feel completely confident about my writing yet to make fully Spanish songs; that's why I write so much bilingual."
- "Being bicultural has always been part of my life."

**Album Personalities:**
- Red Moon in Venus explores her "higher self, speaking [more] from a place of love."
- Orquideas represents "my bitchier side."

## Variety — "Kali Uchis on 'Orquideas': Risktaking, Love and 'Princess Treatment'" (2024)
URL: https://variety.com/2024/music/news/kali-uchis-orquideas-interview-peso-pluma-karol-g-1235861180/

**Genre/Identity:**
- "The scope of how the music industry views Latinas is so boxed, and tied into certain stereotypes."
- "I do feel kind of pigeonholed by the term Latin artist, so I try to challenge that narrative."
- "I'm a Latina who makes music in English and Spanish. Having my artistry pinned down to my ethnicity as if it's a genre is weird."

**Album Concept:**
- "The underlying concept is princess treatment — setting your standards to a certain level."
- "It's about self-love, luxury and divine femininity, with elements of mystery."
- "I loved the juxtaposition of the harshness of the rope and the softness of the flower."
- "The entire theme of this record revolves around that juxtaposition."

**Creative Process:**
- "I already knew how I wanted it to sound, what story I wanted to tell and how I wanted it to play out conceptually."
- "I'm always going to encourage people to try something different."
- "Writing for it has been a super healing process."

## Rolling Stone — "Pop Loner Kali Uchis on Growing Up Punk in Colombia" (Isolation era, 2018)
URL: https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-latin/kali-uchis-isolation-colombia-interview-2018-700772/

**Genre/Sound:**
- "Anytime there's a genre, it's because it has to be classified for uploading to a platform."
- "I'm not looking to make a song that has a beginning or an end as much as projecting a feeling."

**Bilingual Identity:**
- "I was supposed to stay in Colombia my whole life, but my parents decided to move to Virginia."
- "I need to be constantly staying on top of my shit when it comes to my language — otherwise I'm just totally gonna lose it!"

**Creative Process:**
- "The way I work best is still from scratch on the keyboard, when I'm by myself in my room."
- "My music is always just an open diary. I just write whatever I feel."

**Cultural Background:**
- "Colombia is actually very punk! There's a lot of punks, there's a Rasta culture...graffiti everywhere...smoking weed under the bridge. That was my teenage experience."

## Dazed — "How Kali Uchis Crafted Her Singular Cinematic Vision" (Isolation, 2018)
URL: https://www.dazeddigital.com/music/article/39831/1/kali-uchis-isolation-debut-album-interview

**Songwriting:**
- "I can write a song in like, 10 minutes, and record them really fast — my best songs are ones I don't have to struggle with."
- "I really try to balance my feminine and masculine energy when I write."

**Genre/Sound:**
- "I find that love songs can be a bit boring, and a lot of the time people expect a female singer to be singing about love or sex."

**Bilingual:**
- "It's really natural for me to write bilingually. I would love to have more songs that were all fully in Spanish."

**Visual Aesthetic:**
- "I like to experiment with my style and use it as an extension of myself...Show your moods, your thoughts, your actions."
- "People have tried to make me, but at the end of the day, my response is always like, 'this is my song, my video.'"

**Future Direction:**
- "I wanna expand into making films and TV shows, maybe doing creative direction."

## Clash Magazine — "In Conversation: Kali Uchis"
URL: https://www.clashmusic.com/features/in-conversation-kali-uchis/

**Vocal Philosophy:**
- "I don't have the biggest voice. Some of my vocal influences like Sade, Gwen and Kelis don't have huge voices either."
- "I've always been interested in unique voices as opposed to perfect voices."

**Creative Control:**
- "I write my own music, this is all my vision."
- "When you have a strong vision as an artist, it's important to stick with that."
- "I wasn't going to have one of those (record) deals where my creativity is compromised."
- "I'm such a perfectionist, so it feels so good to finally have the record out, because I can't alter it anymore."

**Collaboration:**
- "There's this competitive mentality in music that's quite outdated. We should be able to coexist."
- "I don't want to be in competition with anyone, I always want to one-up myself."

**Growth:**
- "We have to give ourselves the space and the time to grow. That allows you be more intuitive."

## NYLON — "The Metamorphosis Of Kali Uchis" (Isolation, 2018)
URL: https://www.nylon.com/articles/kali-uchis-isolation-interview

**Creative Process:**
- "I started making music by myself and figuring all of that on my own."
- "It was just me in the room with a mic."
- "I let myself do what feels natural."

**Perfectionism:**
- "I feel like I became a perfectionist, and I became really hard on myself."
- "Every project I made, I just threw it out and would start a new project."
- "I just got to the point where I had to put my foot down, to just rip the Band-Aid off."

**Collaborators:**
- "I just wanted the collaborators to be people who are very much in their own world."

**Identity:**
- "I'm not a packaged Barbie doll. I invent myself, and I love to reinvent myself."
- "I didn't want to let myself be constricted by boundaries."

## Remezcla — "Kali Uchis Sin Miedo Breakdown" (2020)
URL: https://remezcla.com/features/music/interview-kali-uchis-sin-miedo-del-amor-y-otros-demonios-breakdown/

**Vocal Authenticity:**
- "That's part of what makes my music so raw and so me is the fact that I didn't go to school to learn it."

- "I'd never done nothing like this in Spanish before. And I never heard a song like this in Spanish before."

- On "vaya con dios": "Something that was a bit more like Portishead...that vibe."

**Collaborations:**
- "I don't really like to have a lot of collaborations on my projects."

## Music Connection — "Q&A with Kali Uchis"
URL: https://www.musicconnection.com/qa-with-kali-uchis/

**Creative Origin:**
- "I didn't feel like I chose to be an artist, I just feel like I was born an artist."
- "Staying true to myself...that's the reason why my fans connected with me."
- "I feel like it's a slap in the face of God to not write music in Spanish."

**Songwriting:**
- "Most of the time, randomly a melody and lyrics will pop into my head."
- "I never really know when inspiration is going to spike, so it's very much a spontaneous process."
- "I revisit it later when I'm in the studio and I sit on it."

**Production:**
- "I was very much on my own...working on perfecting and solidifying myself as an artist."
- "I don't really slip into that sound as much." (re: dancey reggaeton)

**Influences:**
- "I really looked up to women in music who had their own sound."
- "It was always important to find my own voice, to not be too inspired by anyone else."

## Crack Magazine — Aesthetic: Kali Uchis
URL: https://crackmagazine.net/article/aesthetic/aesthetic-kali-uchis/

**Visual Philosophy:**
- "I detest trends or brand names. I prefer to make my own clothes and use style as another creative outlet rather than buy expensive clothes that everyone else wants."

**Cultural Roots:**
- "In Colombia, everyone has old school cars because it's the third world, and every once in a while when I saw a beautiful lowrider it was like spotting a unicorn. I couldn't wait to be the girl inside of one."

**World-Building:**
- "Being raised between Colombia and the U.S has made me see the world in a different light. I was very aware that my present circumstances were always malleable and that there's a whole other world out there, no matter if you feel stuck. You can live so many different lives just inside this one."

## BeatRoute — "Kali Uchis Is Living A Retro Daydream"
URL: https://beatroutemedia.com/kali-uchis-interview/

**Vintage/Retro Philosophy:**
- "I've always loved all things retro. As a teenager, I developed my personal style shopping for secondhand clothes and creating new clothes out of them."
- "I would reinvent myself through those expressions of fashion and play around with vintage styles whether that was the 90s, 80s, 70s or 60s; I just loved being able to take something old and make it new, fresh, modern and make it my own."

**Influences:**
- "I love artists that are free and do what they want to. I've always loved Erykah Badu, Amy Winehouse and Selena."
- "Growing up I was really into all types of experimental music: Jimi Hendrix and really just any type of artist who seemed like they were doing their own thing."

**Bicultural:**
- "I think being bicultural made me very open minded to the world."

## NPR — "Kali Uchis on Red Moon in Venus" (2023)
URL: https://www.npr.org/2023/03/11/1162836538/kali-uchis-on-her-new-album-red-moon-in-venus

**Soul as Genre:**
- "I look at soul as, like, an underlying term for my music. I sing, and I write from my soul, and so I just look at all my music as soul music because of that."

**Album Concept:**
- "The music is very feminine. It's about love, so Venus felt, like, obvious for me. And then red moon — it just felt like the energy."
- "Something very important for me was showing all the different dimensions of love — you know, the downs and the ups and the times where you're at peace, the times where you're in pieces, all of it."

**Influences:**
- "I've always listened to older music. When I was younger people would always get in my iTunes and be like, 'What the fuck is this?'"
- "My grandma got me into Billie Holiday and Ella Fitzgerald while my aunt and uncle got me into Latin soul music."

## AP / Washington Post — "Kali Uchis Pours Her Heart Into 'Sincerely'" (2025)
URL: https://www.washingtonpost.com/entertainment/2025/05/08/kali-uchis-sincerely-new-album-interview/

**Album Concept:**
- "I'm gonna make sure each of these songs is like, this is my letter to the world, this is my letter to this person, this my letter to that, this one to myself."
