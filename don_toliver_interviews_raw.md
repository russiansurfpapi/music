## Cedar Sound Studios — Don Toliver Vocal Chain Analysis
URL: https://www.cedarsoundstudios.com/blogs/news/how-to-sound-like-don-toliver-a-modern-melodic-trap-vibe

**Vocal Production (third-party analysis, not direct quotes):**
- Auto-Tune retune speed: 5-12ms — faster than "natural" but not the instant 0ms snap, creating notes that "bend and wobble in a fluid, almost liquid way"
- Long hall or plate reverb (2-3 second decay) mixed at 30-40% — "way more than most artists" — with heavy low-pass filter at 5-6kHz for "dark and dreamy instead of bright and washy"
- Gentle compression: 3:1, slow attack (15-20ms), auto release — unlike trap artists who compress hard
- Double-tracking vocals, especially hooks, for thick spacey texture
- Harmonies in thirds and fifths, pitch-shifted layers blended quietly for "haunting stereo effect"
- Instrumental context: "dreamy, atmospheric trap beats at slow-to-mid tempos" with "reverb-drenched pads, ambient textures, detuned synths, chopped samples"
