## Nina Kraviz

### Source: Crack Magazine — Nina Kraviz in conversation with James Murphy
URL: https://crackmagazine.net/article/long-reads/nina-kraviz-in-conversation-with-james-murphy-same-same-but-different/

**Genre/Subgenre:**
- "What is techno? To one festival it's one thing and then you take a two-hour flight to a different part of Europe...and it's still techno, but the approach is completely different."
- "The word techno, for each individual, might mean something completely different."
- "Detroit techno wouldn't exist without Italo disco. Even though when you look at Italo disco and Detroit techno you might say what is the connection? But there's a huge connection."

**Set Arc:**
- "I would clear dancefloors at festivals, on purpose sort of"
- "Playing the same record three times, which is something I used to do. Pick up the needle, blow it off"
- "There's a lot of room for energy or drive" but less "room for expansion, for emotion"
- "People are kind of addicted to the adrenaline rush"
- "A good leader has enough power to push through their ideas but at the same time respects the people"
- "Bad sound can ruin a party"

### Source: Crack Magazine — Nina Kraviz Collects Stories with Her trip Label
URL: https://crackmagazine.net/article/long-reads/nina-kraviz-collects-stories-%D1%82%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%BF-label/

**Genre:**
- "For me techno is a period of time, a cultural genre, a feeling and an attitude"
- "the whole vibe around the music, everything electronic, technology. It is an era."
- "I could hear the person behind the track." — Perfect technical execution alone doesn't suffice; emotional authenticity matters more.
- "I like music that is almost a mistake, a coincidence. You plan something and you have an outcome that's completely different."

### Source: XLR8R — 20 Questions: Nina Kraviz
URL: https://xlr8r.com/features/20-questions-nina-kraviz/

**Genre:**
- "Psychedelic rock, dreamy '90s techno from Detroit, New York, Stockholm, and Reykjavik. Dusty house from Chicago. IDM and Italo disco."

**Production:**
- "I connect everything quickly, hit the 'record' button, and start fooling around with a melody or an idea"
- "out of an hour of live recording, I only leave the best mistakes, authentic-sounding sporadic moments in between wonky harmonies"

**Sonic Philosophy:**
- On acid: "It just feels right. Every time I listen to it, it's like aliens have landed and are talking to me."
- "trippy, repetitive, going nowhere patches of words or just a voice that appears for a second and never comes back again... deviating harmonies and a very specific groove"
- "magic place where the DJ, the people, and the room become one"

### Source: DJ Mag — Nina Kraviz: 'Techno is underestimated as a genre'
URL: https://djmag.com/content/nina-kraviz-%E2%80%98techno-underestimated-genre%E2%80%99

- "It's a phenomenon, it's as important as jazz"
- "It's incredibly rich, culturally, but it's so underground that it took me 15 years to have even a little bit of a frame of reference"

## Honey Dijon

### Source: DJ Mag — "I want people to feel like they stepped into another world"
URL: https://djmag.com/features/honey-dijon-i-want-people-feel-they-stepped-another-world

**Genre:**
- "I can go anywhere from a deep house track to a techno track to an Italo-disco track."
- "I'm not a one-genre DJ, I'm not a one-trick pony."

**Set Arc:**
- "For me, it's about storytelling, emotions, tension."
- "You play differently at two than you will at six."
- "You play differently building the room, changing the room, switching the room."
- "The crowd will change over the night."
- "I had residencies where I started at 10 and I played till four in the morning."
- "It's incredibly difficult for me to DJ for 90 minutes because you're playing from a point of entertainment as opposed to the actual craft of DJing."

**Sound:**

- "Loud does not mean good."

### Source: Mixmag (via WebSearch summary)

- "a facilitator of human connection through sound"
- "dance music or house music or techno has gone from culture to entertainment"

## Helena Hauff

### Source: Crack Magazine — Death Disco
URL: https://crackmagazine.net/article/long-reads/helena-hauff-death-disco/

**Genre:**
- "jarring acid to banging techno with infusions of old school industrial, Dutch electronica, post-punk and EBM"
- "rougher, more acidic music"

**Production/Gear:**
- "analog machines" (Juno-60, Roland-303, MPC)
- "aesthetic of machines is so appealing to me"
- "The machine comes to life and becomes something with its own soul"

**Philosophy:**
- "I've always loved it when music...sounds a bit nasty and a bit raw and unpolished"
- "I don't think about music in an emotional way"
- "Proper darkness is a bad place...The rest is just life"

### Source: Fact Magazine — Helena Hauff talks philosophy, politics
URL: https://www.factmag.com/2015/03/07/i-want-to-destroy-society-helena-hauff-talks-philosophy-politics-and-expecting-the-unexpected/

- "grinding analogue techno"
- "gurning electro-techno"
- "krautish zone-outs"
- "a single kick drum, scoured with nauseating waves of distortion, and the odd burst of anguished screaming"
- "I've got more of a punk approach to music -- just do it and get it out there"
- Studied "systematic music science" covering synthesizers and music theory but rejected academic rigor
- "You don't have to study music science to do that. You have to just make music."

### Source: DJ Mag — Helena Hauff: All Night Long
URL: https://djmag.com/features/hauff-music-all-night-long

**Genre:**
- "dark, industrial acid techno which she produces and performs is a niche in itself, a sub-genre of a sub-genre"
- "'90s acid anthems mixed with post-punk off-cuts"
- "minimal wave"

**Production:**
- Uses "hardware" exclusively, rejects computers as "uninspiring and distracting"
- Equipment: TR-303s, 808, MPC-2000, Boss RE-20 pedals, Roland Alpha Juno
- "Vinyl-only DJ sets"
- "30-year-old machines" exclusively
- "tracks are a series of live jams rather than traditional songs"

**Mixing:**
- "Whether I mix them in on the one or two or whatever really depends on the track...I tend to concentrate on the snare/clap"

### Source: XLR8R — Ask the Experts: Helena Hauff

- "I don't wanna force myself into making music...I only do it if I'm really in the mood for it"
- "when I am in the right mood I tend to work really quickly. A lot of the times I jam and let things just happen, but sometimes I have a certain idea in mind"
- "Very 'happy' music seems to sound a bit fake to me sometimes...When I'm feeling a bit down, I'd rather listen to something telling me it's ok to feel like that"

## Objekt (TJ Hertz)

### Source: Crack Magazine — When form becomes protocol, Objekt instinctively resists
URL: https://crackmagazine.net/article/long-reads/when-form-becomes-protocol-objekt-instinctively-resists/

**Genre:**
- "I have a massive love hate relationship with techno"
- The techno scene has "re-solidified into something quite established and monolithic"
- "Though his style falls in line with the original application of the term techno as a genre...he couldn't be further, sonically and ideologically, from its straighter, less ambitious developments."

**Set:**
- "Electro, broken beats and what could be loosely described as thrilling, hybrid transformations of techno are slickly presenting themselves."
- "The pace changes frequently and there is no 'Berghain techno' to be heard"

**Production:**
- Pieces can pass through "as many as 80 different versions until deemed complete"
- "amorphous sounding, more cerebral productions that didn't have any space in clubland at all"

### Source: DJ Mag — Objekt: Into Another Dimension
URL: https://djmag.com/content/objekt-another-dimension

**Genre:**
- "the differences to an outsider between say Detroit techno and electro house are probably tiny, but for people like me, they are huge."
- "It's a genre full of tropes -- the big 808 drums, robot vocals -- but I like the way that a lot of the releases on Shipwrec, Solar One and Frustrated Funk are bringing the weirdness back"
- "What Sandwell District and [Donato] Dozzy were doing seven years ago was really great, but the downside is that it fed this current wave of dreary techno that is coming out now."

**Production:**
- "My working style is one of revision after revision. The tracks start off very rough. It's like chipping away at a block of stone..."
- "I like the idea of making electronic music that you have to think about"
- "I have a soft spot for the metallic, robotic and austere"
- "experimenting is only good if there is some focus, some purpose"
- "I'd much rather if people fucked with formulas instead of buying a distortion pedal."

## Theo Parrish

### Source: Crack Magazine — An impassioned discussion with Detroit icon, Theo Parrish
URL: https://crackmagazine.net/article/long-reads/theo-parrish/

**On Genre Labels:**
- "I've been called 'raw', I've been called 'complex', I've been called 'mean', I've been called 'nice'... I think this labelling happens because everyone and their mama has access to a million and one adjectives."

**Production:**
- "When you're dealing with 3D work, you're dealing with volume, height and density. With sound, you're dealing with almost the same things except you're dealing with a time element too."

**Vinyl:**
- "The music is telling a story. Those pieces of vinyl, they signify effort... If it's existing in the physical universe, it seems as if you care."
- "Everything's transient. The only thing that's valuable is their collection of files. I'm of the generation where if you had something you took care of it."
- "Just because you put it on record didn't mean shit. It had to be good."

**Authenticity:**
- "It should be an honest reflection of the human condition... there's a tendency to make everything slick and pristine and take our human part out of it."

### Source: Fact Magazine (via WebSearch summary)
- "I'm not comfortable with convenience replacing artistry"

## Ame (Frank Wiedemann / Kristian Beyer)

## Cyril Hahn (Swiss/Vancouver-based deep house producer)

### Source: Complex UK — "Cyril Hahn Is The Musical Outsider Everyone Wants To Know" (Oct 2014)
URL: https://www.complex.com/music/2014/10/cyril-hahn-interview

**On aesthetic:**
- "I always needed to have a melody in there. I always preferred the mellow stuff, and still do."
- "I didn't know much about house music I just liked steady kick drums and then everyone started calling my music deep house but I had absolutely no clue what they were talking about."

**On production process:**
- "Writing original is definitely more challenging for me, but it's also way more rewarding in the end once you have an original."
- "It's just completely yours and you know exactly what you've created and that it's something new."
- "Someone might end up sampling your music. And, for me, as someone who started out by remixing, I really love that idea."
- "Even though I'm not remixing anymore, working with accappellas is still my favourite way to work; chopping them up is something I just really enjoy."
- "I would just do it without thinking consequentially."

**On broader ambitions:**
- "I'd love to score a soundtrack. I'm really into ambient post-rock and a lot of instrumental music, so it'd be nice to tie back to that."
- "There's definitely some interesting stuff coming from the underground—at least in the world of electronica which is giving it a whole new dimension."

### Source: Wonderland Magazine — "New Noise: Cyril Hahn" (July 2014)
URL: https://www.wonderlandmagazine.com/2014/07/04/new-noise-cyril-hahn/

**On upbringing / ear:**
- "I've always really been into music – both as a listener and musician – but the realisation that I could pursue music as a career came very late."
- "My parents were really open minded and listened to a lot of different stuff… blues, jazz, classical music, rock…"

**On selection criteria:**
- "It's usual the vocals. I look for hooks or sounds that really stand out to me."
