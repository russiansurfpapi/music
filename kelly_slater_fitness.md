# Kelly Slater — Training & Fitness Directory (Deep Dive)

Compiled from 12+ sources including the Gary Brecka podcast (TUH #235), Joe Rogan Experience #1185, WSL features, CI Surfboards interviews, Stab Magazine, FloGrappling, and The Inertia. July 2026.

---

## Training Philosophy

Slater's core principle is strategic under-training — maintaining peak function with minimum wear:

- "I don't overexert myself very much. My theory on longevity is like 'don't overdo it'. I don't need necessarily to be over-trained for what I do."
- "The oldest people in the world weren't athletes who burned themselves out too much."
- "I'm kind of like a bench workout person where I get into it and then I'm lazy." — admits he's not naturally disciplined about gym work
- "It's all about where your mind's at." — mental state as the foundation of fitness
- "I don't know anyone in the world who is becoming the best at something without being obsessive, because they love it."
- "When you first make a change you're in control of with your body, it's so exciting and inspiring."

He competed professionally from age 19 to 52, winning his 11th world title at 39 and his last event at 50. No one else on tour has ever been within 8 years of his age.

## Water Training

### Surfing (Primary Conditioning)
- 3-5 hours daily in water when in Hawaii — this IS his cardio
- Describes surfing as providing natural cardiovascular conditioning without structured gym work
- After competitions, takes up to two weeks completely off surfing to recover
- Spent roughly half his days in the water throughout his career
- When conditions are excellent, skips eating entirely to get straight into the water
- "The best way to wake up" — entering the ocean is his morning activation

### Pool / Underwater Training (XPT with Laird Hamilton)
- Participated in Laird Hamilton and Gabby Reece's XPT (Extreme Performance Training) in Malibu
- Pool sessions include underwater exercises with weights (dumbbell walks on pool floor, underwater lunges)
- Combined with Wim Hof breathing → ice bath immersion → max push-ups protocol
- XPT format: breathwork → pool exercises → ice bath → beach workout → nutrition guidance
- The pool work is designed for hold-under wipeout preparation and nervous system conditioning

### Surf Ranch (Wave Pool)
- Designed and built his own artificial wave at Lemoore, California
- Allows repeatable practice on identical waves — fundamentally changed surf preparation
- Enables targeted training on specific maneuvers without waiting for ocean conditions
- Continues upgrading the technology; used for WSL competition events

## Gym / Weight Training

Slater does NOT follow a structured gym program. His cross-training is varied and sport-based:

### Primary Cross-Training Activities
- **Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu** — Blue belt. Trains no-gi. Has trained with Rickson Gracie, Kron Gracie, the Mendes brothers (Art of Jiu-Jitsu), Mario Sperry, Vitor Belfort, and Marcus "Buchecha" Almeida. "There's something about jiu-jitsu that puts you in your place. Even if you're the top guy, you still have to teach little kids. If you have insecurities, they get worked out on the mat."
- **Boxing** — occasional training, not documented in detail
- **Golf** — plays regularly; significant activity in his current routine. Notes it can aggravate his plantar fasciitis from the push-off motion
- **Beach running** — occasional, not structured

### Bodyweight Exercises
- Push-ups (documented as primary strength exercise)
- Push-ups done as part of XPT protocol: max reps after Wim Hof breathing, before ice bath
- Core work integrated through surfing movement patterns rather than isolated ab exercises

### What He Does NOT Do
- No heavy barbell training
- No structured weightlifting program
- No gym membership or regular gym attendance
- No bodybuilding-style hypertrophy work
- "I don't need necessarily to be over-trained for what I do" — explicitly rejects high-volume training

## Diet & Nutrition

### Evolution
- **Childhood**: "Lots of sugar and ice cream" — Oreos, Doritos, cupcakes, chocolate milkshakes with Hershey syrup
- **Age ~20**: Discovered Dr. Joel Wallach's teachings on nutritional deficiency. Began self-educating about how "foods work inside your body"
- **Mid-20s onward**: Learned food combining from fellow surfer Tom Carroll, who introduced protein-carb separation principles
- **Current**: Clean, plant-forward, food-combining practitioner. Not strictly vegan but heavily plant-based

### Morning Routine
- Wakes between 7-8 AM (not an early riser; prefers staying up late)
- Starts with **hot water and lemon** to cleanse the body after sleep
- Breakfast: fruit-based meals, particularly smoothies
- Smoothie base: almond milk or coconut milk + frozen fruit + sprouts + greens
- "Greens digest with fruits pretty readily" — his rule for combining in smoothies
- Chia seeds soaked overnight in coconut water with berries and nuts is another breakfast option
- "If you can get yourself into a pattern where the best, healthiest foods are the things you enjoy, your life is going to be better"

### Food Combining Rules (Trophology)
Learned from reading about Rickson Gracie's diet, which led him to "The Tao of Health, Sex, and Longevity" by Daniel Reid:

1. **Never mix protein and carbs** — they require different acids and alkalis for digestion. Combining them forces the stomach to produce both simultaneously, neutralizing each other and slowing digestion
2. **Don't mix citrus with non-acidic fruits** — different enzyme requirements
3. **Greens combine well with fruit** — can be added to smoothies
4. **Don't eat too many different types of foods together** — simplicity aids digestion
5. **Vary protein types daily** — "vary the types of amino acids you are eating" rather than eating the same protein repeatedly

When strictly practicing food combining: reports sleeping only 6 hours per night with sustained high energy all day. Improved digestion, better sleep quality, eliminated stomach problems.

### Staple Foods
- **Chia seeds** — soaked overnight, added to smoothies, mixed into morning tea. Rich in omega-3, protein, fiber, antioxidants
- **Coconut water** — primary hydration (sponsored by Vita Coco)
- **Almonds** — carries as a snack; observed eating between competition heats
- **Avocados** — eaten between semifinals; healthy fat source
- **Kombucha tea** — replaced most caffeine intake. Fermented, probiotic-rich
- **Fresh-pressed juices** — sipped throughout the day
- **Steak** — not vegetarian; eats grass-fed, organic beef (higher omega-3, lower fat than grain-fed)
- **Fish tacos** — "a staple for surfers"
- **Kimchi** — fermented probiotic food
- **Fruits** — prioritized in mornings; freezes fresh fruit (collected 50 mini bananas from a fallen tree in Hawaii to freeze)

### What He Avoids
- Most grains and white flour
- Gluten (avoids "for the most part")
- Excessive caffeine (replaced with kombucha)
- Processed foods — reads every ingredient panel
- Mixing many food types in one meal

### Fasting Protocols
- **Water fasting** advocate: "three days minimum to get past day two" for meaningful results. "Water fasting is game changer"
- Completed 4-day fasts while maintaining full energy for surfing, golf, and beach workouts
- Day 2 described as the hardest; Day 3 brings mental clarity and sustained energy
- Completed 10-day **Master Cleanse** protocols with cayenne pepper tea
- Reports dramatic energy and eyesight improvements during extended fasts
- Recommends medical supervision for fasts beyond 7 days

### Purps (His Own Drink Brand)
- Co-founded with PM Tenore and Dr. Chris Schaumburg
- Formulated from: water, coconut water, organic "Purple Life Formula" (mangosteen, maqui berry, acai, blueberry, grape, blackberry)
- Sweetened with organic evaporated cane sugar
- Three formulas: Hydro, Fuel, Vita
- No artificial colors, flavors, or preservatives

## Recovery & Longevity at 54

### Recovery Protocols
- **Cold plunging** — calls it "lazy man's way to work out" for its cardiovascular benefits without physical strain
- **Sauna sessions** — regular use, especially post-massage
- **Deep tissue massage** — multiple times weekly. "Go until I scream." Reports improved range of motion within 24 hours
- **Hot baths/sauna post-massage** with electrolyte supplementation
- **Red light therapy** — integrated into sleep environment
- **PEMF (Pulsed Electromagnetic Field) beamer mat** — uses during sleep for recovery
- **Grounding/earthing** — "touching the surface of the earth is one of the healthiest things"
- Ocean immersion for negative ions and ozone exposure

### Major Injuries & Recovery
- **Hip**: Labrum tear at age 19 — surfed 30+ years on the torn labrum, won world championships on it. Total hip replacement at age ~53 (2024). Surgery involved labrum reconstruction, scar tissue removal, bone spur removal on femoral head, socket joint shaving, and removing loose bone fragments
- **Foot (2017)**: Multiple metatarsal fractures requiring two surgeries, 16 screws, 3 plates. Return to competition in 5 months. Secondary plantar plate tear in May 2018 — chose no surgical intervention. Residual phantom nerve pain and recurring plantar fasciitis
- **Functional scoliosis**: Discovered via Prenova body scan (June 2024). The curved spine actually benefited performance in certain surfing situations — his body adapted around the asymmetry

### Longevity Philosophy
- Wallach's principle: "No one dies from old age — you die of a deficiency of something"
- Strategic rest: up to 2 weeks off surfing after competitions
- Under-training rather than over-training: retains strength without burnout
- "I just got addicted to something healthy" — framing health practices as positive addiction
- Plans continued competition in select events into his 60s potentially
- "I wanna be healthy and get barreled at Backdoor when I'm 90 years old. What's your goal?"

### Sleep
- Targets 8 hours nightly with early wake times (7-8 AM)
- Prefers staying up late — not naturally a morning person
- When practicing strict food combining, sleeps only ~6 hours with full energy
- Uses PEMF beamer mat and red light therapy in sleep environment

## Flexibility & Mobility

### Yoga
- Listed among his regular activities alongside surfing, BJJ, boxing, and golf
- Part of his consistent "training + stretching + nutrition" triangle
- Not documented as following a specific yoga style or teacher

### Stretching
- Stretching sessions integrated post-massage
- Multiple times per week, seamlessly combined with breathwork
- Emphasized as one of the three pillars (with training and nutrition) that keeps his body strong and injury-free

### Breathwork: Wim Hof Method
- Practices the Wim Hof breathing technique. Was publicly spotted doing it before his Round 4 heat at the Fiji Pro:
  1. Lay on back or sit in meditation position
  2. 30 steady power breaths (~3 minutes)
  3. On last breath, inhale deeply, exhale completely
  4. Hold breath until the body forces you to breathe
  5. Repeat 3-4 cycles
- Uses it for nervous system control and pre-competition preparation
- **Caution**: Slater has passed out during a breathing session — he acknowledges the intensity and risk
- Combines breathwork with cold exposure (ice baths) as part of XPT protocol

### Jiu-Jitsu as Mobility
- BJJ provides both flexibility work and body awareness
- "There's something about jiu-jitsu that puts you in your place" — uses it for humility and mental grounding as much as physical conditioning
- Prefers no-gi training
- Wishes he had the same analytical depth in BJJ as he does in surfing: "In jiu-jitsu I can't do that, and I really wish I had that knowledge"

## Training Evolution: 20s → 50s

| Era | Approach |
|---|---|
| **Teens–Early 20s** | Pure surfing. Junk food diet. No structured training. Natural talent and youth |
| **Mid-20s** | Diet awakening via Dr. Joel Wallach. Began food combining. Cut sugar drastically |
| **Late 20s–30s** | Added BJJ (met Rickson ~1998). Food combining became core practice. Kombucha replaced caffeine |
| **Late 30s** | Won 11th world title at 39. Added XPT with Laird Hamilton. Wim Hof breathwork |
| **40s** | Oldest competitor on tour by 8+ years. Foot injury (2017) requiring 16 screws, 3 plates. Built Surf Ranch wave pool |
| **50s** | Water fasting protocols. PEMF + red light therapy for recovery. Hip replacement at ~53. Comeback at Backdoor Shootout just before 54th birthday. Golf became significant activity. Cold plunging as "lazy man's workout" |

The trend: as he aged, formal training DECREASED while recovery technology, dietary sophistication, and strategic rest INCREASED. He competes less but recovers more deliberately.
